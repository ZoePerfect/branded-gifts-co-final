Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Branded Gifts Co LLC ("BRANDED GIFTS CO," "we," "us," or "our") operates this website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you submit a project inquiry form, contact us by email, or communicate with us through other channels. This may include your name, company name, email address, phone number, website, whether you are a brand or a distributor, project details, and any artwork files you choose to send.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide quotes
  • To communicate about your project
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties except as necessary to fulfill your project requirements or as required by law.

Cookies

Our website may use standard cookies and analytics tools to improve user experience and understand website performance. You may adjust your browser settings to decline cookies, though some website features may be affected.

Website Analytics

We may use third-party analytics tools to better understand how visitors use our website and improve our services. This data is collected in aggregate and does not personally identify individual visitors.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@brandedgiftsco.com.
