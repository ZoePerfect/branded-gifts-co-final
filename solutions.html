01

Turnkey Gift Kits

You share the goal, audience and budget. We deliver a finished, branded kit to your door, with every step handled in between.

Best forMarketing, HR and events teams planning welcome kits, client appreciation gifts, event and conference kits, and holiday gifting.
Typical quantity100–5,000 kits
Typical timeline3–6 weeks

What we handle

  • Concept and product curation built around your brand and budget
  • Visual mockups of the full kit before you commit
  • Sourcing, logo printing, engraving and embroidery
  • Custom gift box, inserts and thank-you card
  • Sample, or photo and video approval for rush timelines
  • Quality control, kit assembly and packing
  • DDP delivery: shipping, duties and customs included
Example conceptFor a produce distributor, we designed a chef gift for its customers. The kit: a natural canvas drawstring pouch, three acacia-wood silicone kitchen tools with a laser-engraved logo, and a custom thank-you card, designed to fit a fixed budget and delivered in time for the holidays.
02

OEM & ODM Manufacturing

Custom products made at volume, under your brand. Use an existing design with your logo (OEM), or develop something new from your idea (ODM).

Best forBrands launching private-label products, retail and e-commerce lines, and large corporate merchandise programs.
Typical quantity1,000–50,000+ units
Typical timeline4–10 weeks

What we handle

  • Factory matching across drinkware, bags, apparel, tech, home, candles and more
  • Product development, tooling and custom colors (Pantone)
  • Private label, retail packaging and barcodes
  • Pre-production samples and in-line quality inspections
  • Third-party testing arranged for the US and EU (e.g., CPSIA, Prop 65, FDA/LFGB food contact, REACH)
  • Consolidated shipping with DDP or FOB options
Example scenarioA lifestyle brand turns a best-selling tumbler into its own private-label line: custom color, laser-engraved logo, food-contact testing and retail-ready gift boxes, shipped DDP to its US warehouse.
03

Kitting & Fulfillment

Already have products or suppliers? Need kits sent one by one to hundreds of addresses? We assemble, store and ship, so you don't have to.

Best forEmployee onboarding programs, crowdfunding rewards, influencer seeding and PR mailers, and any project with many delivery addresses.
Typical quantityFrom 100 kits
ShippingBulk, split or individual

Three flexible options

We source and kit

We supply every item, then brand, assemble and pack the complete kit.

Bring your own suppliers

Your suppliers ship to us. We receive, count and inspect the goods, then assemble and pack the kits, and report any issue before it reaches your customer.

Store and ship on demand

We hold finished kits and ship them individually from your address list, to one country or many, as new employees join or orders come in.

  • Split deliveries to multiple locations
  • Individual shipments from a simple spreadsheet, with tracking
  • Size and variant sorting (e.g., T-shirt sizes per recipient)
  • Personalized cards and inserts
04

Custom Packaging

The unboxing is the first impression. We make branded packaging that looks as considered as what's inside.

Best forBoutique brands, clinics and studios, PR and influencer boxes, and anyone upgrading their customer experience.
Typical quantityFrom 100–500 pcs
Typical timeline2–4 weeks

What we make

  • Rigid and magnetic gift boxes
  • Mailer and shipping boxes
  • Drawer and presentation cases
  • Custom inserts (paper, foam, EVA)
  • Branded tissue paper
  • Stickers, labels and seals
  • Thank-you and greeting cards
  • Pouches and drawstring bags

Finishes include foil stamping, embossing and debossing, spot UV, soft-touch and matte lamination, and Pantone matching. Digital proofs are provided before production.

For promotional products distributors: your factory-direct production team behind the scenes. You keep the client relationship, and we make you look good.

ShippingBlind and drop-ship
